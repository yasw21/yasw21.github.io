Approach

A repeatable growth engine

I run experimentation as an always-on programme rather than one-off tests, with prioritisation, documentation and stakeholder read-outs built in.

01 / Discover

Find the friction

Analyse funnels in GA4, Adobe Analytics, Looker Studio and SQL, and review session recordings and user research.

02 / Hypothesise

Build the case

Write hypotheses grounded in evidence and tied to a metric that matters to the business.

03 / Prioritise

Focus on impact

Score ideas with ICE and PIE to balance impact, confidence, effort and dependencies.

04 / Build & QA

Ship it properly

Build variations in Optimizely with HTML, CSS and JS, and QA them across browsers, devices and regions.

05 / Analyse

Read it rigorously

Analyse results statistically and segment them by market, since the same change can win in one country and lose in another.

06 / Scale

Roll out & iterate

Turn results into commercial recommendations, roll out the winners, and feed what we learned into the next round of tests.
